Purdue automatic sign-in

Settings stay in this Chrome profile. Anyone with access to this profile may be able to recover the password and authenticator secret.

Set up automatic sign-in

  1. Keep this unpacked extension folder in place. After updating its files, open chrome://extensions and click Reload.
  2. At Microsoft Security info, sign in with your existing method. Choose + Add sign-in method → Microsoft Authenticator → I want to use a different authenticator app → Can’t scan QR Code? Purdue’s guide shows this path. A URL beginning mobileappcommunicator.auth.microsoft.com/activatev2 means you are in Microsoft Authenticator setup; go back and choose the different-app link.
  3. Copy the Secret key into the field below. Enter your career account and password, choose your campus, enable automatic sign-in, then save. Use the displayed current code to verify the new method on Microsoft’s page. Keep your old method until verification succeeds.
  4. Start sign-in from Purdue Brightspace or another Purdue service. Use the toolbar popup to choose Regular automatic sign-in, Switch accounts, or Complete manual. Switch accounts signs out and returns to your page, selects your campus, then waits for you to choose an account. If you choose your saved account, automatic sign-in continues; another account stays manual for that sign-in. Complete manual lasts 15 minutes, 30 minutes, one hour, or until you resume it, and asks whether to sign out now. The popup also has a separate Sign out button.

Sign-out ends Brightspace and the saved Microsoft account session, then holds automatic sign-in until you choose it again. Purdue SSO may still retain a session. If switching accounts skips the picker or MFA, clearing site data only for Purdue sign-in and Microsoft sign-in sites is a fallback to test later; this extension does not clear cookies.

A current six-digit code is not the setup key. Never share the setup key in chat or a pull request.
